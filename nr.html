Prepare your Form 1040-NR (2025)

For nonresident alien students with a campus job or taxable scholarship. Everything runs on your device: your documents and answers are processed only here, are never sent to anyone, and are never stored on a server. When you finish, you get one PDF to print, sign, and mail. Nothing is filed for you.

My1040NR is a free, open source project. It is not affiliated with the IRS. Preparing a form is not the same as filing it: print, sign, and mail the PDF yourself. Source code on GitHub.
