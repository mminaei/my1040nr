The problem we fix

Wrong form, real trouble

Most international students are nonresident aliens for tax purposes. Mainstream software files Form 1040 for residents. Filing the wrong form can mean a wrong refund, a lost treaty benefit, and amended returns later.

Every F and J holder must file something

Even with zero US income, F, J, M and Q holders (and their dependents) must mail Form 8843 every year. Most students never hear this until a university email scares them in March.

Money left on the table

Treaty exemptions, tax-free scholarship rules, and FICA taxes your employer should never have withheld. A student-only product checks all three, every time.

How it works

1. Residency first

We run the residency day test for your visa: 5 calendar years for F and J students, 2 years for J scholars and teachers. We never ask you if you were "exempt". We ask your first year, count your years for you, and show you the day math, not a black box.

2. Documents you actually get

W-2, Form 1042-S from your university, 1099s, and your I-94 travel history. We explain why your school sent both a W-2 and a 1042-S, and we stop you from entering the same income twice.

3. The right forms, ready to file

Form 1040-NR with Schedules NEC and OI, Form 8843 for you and your dependents, Form 8833 when your treaty claim needs it, delivered as one PDF you download. Page 1 of that PDF is your mailing slip: your return address, the correct IRS address for your exact return in large type, a checklist of what goes in the envelope, and a cut-out label for the envelope. We prepare the forms. You sign and mail them. We never transmit anything to the IRS.

Questions students ask us

Can I use TurboTax?

If you are a nonresident alien, mainstream resident software does not prepare Form 1040-NR. If our checker says you are a resident for tax purposes, resident software can work for you, and we will tell you that honestly.

I earned nothing. Do I still file?

Yes, Form 8843, if you were in the US on an F, J, M or Q visa during the year. It is an information form, not a tax bill.

What is a 1042-S?

The form your university uses to report treaty-exempt pay and taxable scholarships. If you have one, you need a product that reads it correctly. Most do not.

Do I need an SSN?

For Form 8843 alone, no. To file a 1040-NR with income, you need an SSN or an ITIN. We walk you through Form W-7 if you need an ITIN.

My1040NR is tax preparation software, not a tax advisor. For complex cases (dual-status years, treaty clawbacks, prior years filed on the wrong form), we flag the issue and recommend a qualified tax professional who works with nonresident aliens.
